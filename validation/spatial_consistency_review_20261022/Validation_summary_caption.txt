Candidate supplementary figure. Bias (satellite minus reference) and root mean square error of CLARA-A3 and APP-x albedo against each field dataset, ordered chronologically by campaign. Circles denote CLARA-A3 and triangles APP-x. Counts at right give the retained matched pairs for each product; these are not necessarily independent observations. No APP-x estimates are plotted for ARISE because no pairs were retained. The summary uses the current Figure 9 matchups: IDW with valid nearest-neighbor spatial fallback within the existing distance limits, corrected MOSAiC tower sea-ice-concentration blending, and unchanged prior observation selection and temporal matching. Products may have different dates and sample counts, so differences between scores also reflect sampling differences. Metrics are shown in albedo units.
